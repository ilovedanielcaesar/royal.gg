Cut from the redesign — not shipping

The submitted status & review queue

The schema in src/types/database.ts still defines status: 'draft' | 'submitted' | 'approved' and SessionStatusBadge renders "Waiting on admin". In this v2 design, the submit step is removed so any member can edit drafts until locked; implementing this in the real app requires a database migration.

Native <input type="date">

Cut in favor of a hand-rolled playing-card calendar that preserves the felt and card language, complete with full keyboard navigation.

Guest selection in member picker

Guests are segregated from the registered member attendance chips and can only be introduced through the explicit guest search box.

Cent-level manual cashout stepping

The stepper moves exclusively by $1.00 (100 cents), preventing micro-cent fiddling and rounding errors during table counts.

Arithmetic check: The 4 Sep night balances buy-ins of $1,240.00 against reported cash-outs of $1,235.50 (off by $4.50). The real reconcile algorithm allocates +$2.80, +$1.04, +$0.47, and +$0.19 to the four winners, summing to exactly $4.50. Guests added in session editing receive a blank spade card on cream face; in read-only rosters they appear with the felt-dark diamond tile. All figures are sample data.

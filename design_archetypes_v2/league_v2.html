Head-to-head records

The schema records per-night totals, not hands or positions. “You beat Priya 12–8” would only mean out-netting her on shared nights—a weaker claim than promised.

Deleting a player who has played

Deletion is restricted to accounts with zero buy-ins. Removing a player with results would destroy part of the permanent ledger record.

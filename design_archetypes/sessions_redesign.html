Design notes
01

Cut from the redesign — not shipping

The ledger stays deliberately narrow: it shows what the current schema and permissions can support honestly.

A calendar / heatmap view

The group plays weekly on one weekday; a calendar would turn 24 useful rows into one column of dots surrounded by empty cells.

Filtering by player

That view already exists, with better context, on each player’s profile page.

A search box

There are 24 rows. Come back at 200, when search would reduce work instead of adding it.

Editing inline from the ledger

Submitted logs are locked to members. Widening that window needs migrations to the sessions, buy_ins and cash_outs write policies—and answers on who may edit and whether an edit returns the log to draft.

A “next game” row

There is no scheduling or RSVP concept in the schema, and the dashboard already cut the same idea for that reason.

    Who counts as a league member

    status = 'active', profile_id is not null, and not is_guest produce different tables. This mock uses active + played for the ranking, preserves guests outside it, and leaves the product-wide call open.

    Head-to-head records

    The schema records per-night totals, not hands or positions. “You beat Priya 12–8” could only mean out-netting her on shared nights—a narrower, weaker claim than the phrase promises.

    Deleting a player who has played

    Deletion works only when there are no buy-ins, by design. Removing a player with results would destroy part of the permanent record.

    Inviting by email

    Members use the standing join code and its /join/CODE link. Expiring, limited-use group_invites remain admin-only with no select policy in 0010_group_join.sql.

    A season table

    There is no season concept in the data. Recent form windows by five group sessions, and its dated subtitle keeps that claim honest.

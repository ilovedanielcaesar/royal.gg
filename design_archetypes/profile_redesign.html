Merging this page with account-level /profile

QoL item 6: two pages, 197 and 188 lines, read as one. Blocked on GROUPS §11: stats are per-group, and different stakes do not sum honestly for someone in two groups. That is why this page names Thursday Night.

Head-to-head records

The schema stores night totals, not hands or positions. “You beat Priya 12–8” could only mean out-netting her on shared nights—a weaker claim than the phrase promises.

Achievements or badges

Nothing defines an achievement, and the player rating already compresses the record into a single figure.

Linking biggest win and loss

playerStats() returns the amounts but not session ids. The dashboard had to carry the id alongside too. Cheap, but it belongs in stats.ts, not a page mock.

A goal or target for the season

There is no season concept and no per-player target. The dashboard cut the same thing for the same reason.

Uploading a photo

Arithmetic check: the 24 nightly nets sum to exactly +$412.00. The record is 15W–9L, and 15 ÷ 24 is 62.5%. Dividing 41,200 cents by 24 gives 1,716⅔ cents, which rounds up once to +$17.17; +$17.17 × 24 = +$412.08, the eight-cent difference is the visible consequence of rounding the mean. The sample standard deviation is $48.53. The 8.1 rating is playerRating() on this same data: 62.5% win rate, $48.53 session standard deviation, +$98.00 over the last six, and +$412.00 lifetime, weighted 0.25 / 0.20 / 0.20 / 0.35. Dan’s own extrema are +$120.00 / −$62.50. These are internally consistent sample figures, not the real league.
